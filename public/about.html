About CanBot

CanBot is a Canadian weather-alert monitoring system designed to make important weather information simple, accessible, and easy to understand.

⚠️ CanBot Beta

This project is still in Beta and may experience bugs. Please refer to the Contact page to report a bug or a problem with the site.

What is CanBot?

CanBot monitors weather alerts across Canada and organizes them into a simple live-chat style interface. Alerts can be viewed by individual provinces and territories, allowing you to quickly see what is happening in a particular area.

What CanBot Does

🌦️

Weather Alerts

Displays active Canadian weather alerts and important information associated with them.

🇨🇦

Canadian Coverage

CanBot is designed around Canada's provinces and territories.

📍

Province Tabs

Quickly switch between provinces and territories to view regional alerts.

⏱️

Live Alerts

Alerts are updated automatically, and expired alerts are removed from the live display.

⚠️

Alert Icons

Different weather events can be represented with recognizable icons to make alerts easier to identify.

🗺️

CLC System

CanBot uses Canadian forecast-zone information as part of its geographic alert system.
